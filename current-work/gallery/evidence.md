# Gallery evidence

[Gallery foyer](README.md) · [Current asset](../README.md) · [Full image manifest](image-manifest.json)

The curator inspected all 20 selected original images. Eighteen original-image hashes matched their source receipts. The V04 pair is explicitly weaker: the source manifest and successful historical delivery identify those files, but do not supply original PNG hashes; the gallery records independently computed hashes of the inspected files.

No original render is newly generated here. Display JPEGs/thumbnails reduce transfer size from 26.5 MB of originals to about 3.6 MB combined. They preserve framing but are not pixel-identical evidence. The manifest records original and derivative hashes, dimensions, source hashes and transformation recipe. Source model binaries and the private working archive remain external.

**Dates:** indexed 2026-10-29. The record-set dates identify the documentation family, not an inferred original render timestamp. No filesystem mtime or Library upload time is relabeled as a capture time.
