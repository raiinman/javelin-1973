## How to explore

Click an exhibit, then any thumbnail for its individual render card. Every card has a larger display image, checkpoint/source evidence, limits and previous/next links. Follow the numbered rooms or jump straight to a detail.

**Current source bytes** identifies the adopted Library 36 source. **Historical** identifies an earlier checkpoint or inspection state, not a new picture of today's model. Before/after pairs are matched only where their captions say so.

Gallery indexed **2026-10-29**. Record-set dates identify the source documentation family; original render timestamps are unrecorded in the inspected receipts. They are not inferred from file modification times. All images are optimized display derivatives. No crop, retouch, AI redraw or geometry change was used to create this gallery.
