## Top three blocking features

1. **Front bucket silhouette, padding transitions and upholstery volume.** Broad regular backs and tray-like pans dominate whole-cabin clay; the target's channel close-up and several manual/automatic supplemental seats consistently demand a more organic upholstered form. Missing physical bases reinforce the floating impression. Existing support/pattern direction is valuable but not sufficient.
2. **Dashboard/steering assembly still has overly regular major forms and sparse lower hierarchy.** Preserve the improved brow/cheek/handle and original manual/metal-wheel/dial work, but refine their relative silhouette, column/boss depth and control/trim layering in a future dedicated pass. No global dashboard scaling or numerical wheel correction is justified from these photographs alone.
3. **Lower and rear-side cabin remains incomplete.** Plain tunnel/oval boot, no separate seat supports, no belts/buckles and no dedicated quarter interior cards leave the cabin less grounded and enclosed than the references. The precise target console and belt stowage remain unknown; record that uncertainty instead of adopting Druk's automatic assembly.
