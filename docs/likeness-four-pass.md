## 2. Front volume

Video frames around 0:02 and 0:30 show rounded front shoulders, broad upper fenders and a hood stamping integrated into their volume. Compared directly with the current front and quarter renders, the model's crowns read as narrow rails beside a broad hood. The generator retracts the upper fender approximately68mm from the widest side at the front axle, leaving only98mm between its shoulder and the hood boundary. The side/top tangent also changes abruptly. Rebuild that transition as a sampled curved section and inspect reflections, both front quarters and the front silhouette. Keep original hood/fender graphics attached to the actual revised triangles.

## 4. Restored-car trim and Cardin forms

Original video frames around0:08,0:16,0:22,0:53 and1:18 distinguish the restored car's details. Tail bezels are thin bright surrounds around recessed lenses, not thick white blocks. Its sheet metal curves around the tail face and latch area. Cardin fabric follows padded seats; correct ribbon direction alone does not make rectangular seat blocks accurate. A [firsthand 1973 Cardin interior photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1973_AMC_Javelin_AMX_in_original_Cardin_trim_and_401_Go_Package_with_4-speed_at_2017_AMO_meet_3of5.jpg) is supplemental evidence for seat and door forms, not a colour or option override for the supplied car.

## Section order and gates

1. Front body: rounded fender/hood junctions, preserved panel seams and graphic projection, multi-angle source comparison, hinge/steering clearance.
2. Cabin and rear body: continuous door/quarter sections and roof/sail tangents, preserved glass/apron and trunk joins, multi-angle comparison and all panel sweeps.
3. Exterior trim: thin recessed tail surrounds and shaped mirrors/bumpers, reference comparison and outward winding.
4. Cabin padding: shaped cushions/backrests with their Cardin ribbons attached to the actual surfaces, seat registration and interior visibility.

These are working sections, not claims that 1:1 fidelity has been achieved. Native game inspection remains a separate required gate. Geometry decisions must be retained in the generator and rebuilt into the editable Blender source.
