## Baseline measurements and disagreements

The existing [factory-source table](research-1973.md#2-dimensions-exterior-and-powertrain) records wheelbase 110.0 in (2.7940 m), length 192.3 in (4.8844 m), dealer-book width 75.3 in (1.9126 m), height 51.2 in (1.3005 m), and front/rear overhang 42.4/39.8 in. The sales brochure gives width 75.2 in. Keep the disagreement and rounded-dimension limits. These are research constraints, not a fresh measurement or certification of this model.

The target restored car's five-spoke wheels, red rear face, side pipes, white manual knob and visible 360 air-cleaner label must not be replaced simply because supplemental cars differ. The Druk record preserves conflicting sales VIN text versus photographed identification; do not resolve that by guessing.

## Where photographs and PDFs are

At the reviewed base commit, the repository contains research text with direct video/gallery/photo/PDF citations, original model renders and authored product artwork. It contains **no committed actual-car photograph/PDF reference archive**. The cited sources and historical local captures are different resources. Some research reports name ignored `build/` captures/contact sheets; those paths do not make the files available in a clone. This is a repository inventory finding, not a claim that the originals are lost or absent elsewhere.

Keep external source attribution and license restrictions. Viewing access does not grant redistribution rights. Do not add brochure pixels, raw reference captures or unrelated donor-mod geometry/textures to a product package. Preserve existing research files and DOX's [MIT notice](licenses/DOX-MIT.txt).
