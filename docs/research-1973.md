# 1973 Javelin AMX Pierre Cardin research

Four research passes, reviewed 2026-10-17. Factory literature establishes the baseline; the supplied restored car establishes the mod's individual appearance. References are viewing sources, not packaged artwork.

## 1. Identity and equipment

AMC identifies the 1973 Javelin AMX as a four-place two-door hardtop. Cardin fabric trim covers seats, door panels and headliner, with front-fender emblems. It is separate from engine and Go-package choices. The AMX's rear deck spoiler is standard equipment. [AMC sales brochure, PDF pages 13–15](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/1973-AMC-Full-Line-V1.pdf#page=13).

This supports a low spoiler integrated with the deck's rear edge. It does not support adding a tall pedestal wing or treating Cardin trim as a mechanical package.

Its stock slot wheels and optional 15×7/E60×15 combination describe factory configurations, not proof of the video car's wheel size. [AMC sales brochure, pages 14–15](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/1973-AMC-Full-Line-V1.pdf#page=14).

AMC's T-10 service table lists 2.23/1.77/1.35/1.00 and reverse 2.26. This does not identify the restored car's actual gearbox. [1973 service manual scan archive, enclosed page 6-3-7](https://www.amcjavelin.nl/all/javelinfo/Man_08_AMC_Manual_Transmissions.zip).

Use the factory dimensions as independent constraints on the authored body. The original rear overhang required correction; preserve axle spacing instead of uniformly scaling the whole car. Matching dimensions alone does not establish the correct silhouette or flared body sections.

## 3. Cardin interior

The factory illustration shows nested ribbons turning around the seat contours, coordinated door inserts and striped headliner. Flat horizontal bars alone do not capture that treatment. The AMX also has a three-spoke sports wheel and engine-turned instrument panel. [AMC sales brochure, pages 13–14](https://xr793.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/1973-AMC-Full-Line-V1.pdf#page=14).

A firsthand [Cardin headliner photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:AMC_Javelin_Cardin_interior_um-head.jpg) corroborates the ceiling treatment. The general Javelin dashboard illustration must not override the AMX-specific instrument-panel description.

Use original geometry and colours sampled by visual judgment from the supplied video. Preserve the cream, plum and magenta relationships seen there. Sales descriptions of other cars vary in colour terminology and are not paint or fabric measurements.

## 4. The supplied restored car

The original page identifies Bob La Cross as uploader, publication on 2012-09-16, and restoration in 2009. The available transcript supplies no useful spoken specifications. [Original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZpfnHMBiRvA).

Visible evidence: red body and rear face, black hood T graphic, silver mesh grille, round inner grille lamps, five-spoke wheels, slotted side exhausts, white manual-shift knob, Cardin upholstery and a 360-labelled air cleaner. The rear close-up shows pale inserts only in the inner lamps. These observations establish the depicted configuration; they do not authenticate engine internals, VIN options, paint code or factory originality.

Retain that visible configuration. Do not substitute another auction car's gold stripe, 401 engine, stock slot wheels or black rear panel. The [Mecum comparison car](https://www.mecum.com/lots/502692/) has different equipment and is only a supplemental body-shape reference.

## Implementation consequences

- Preserve the correct wheelbase while shortening excess rear overhang by approximately 119 mm, using shared tail stations rather than scaling the whole car.
- Keep the seated AMX spoiler, inner-only reverse inserts and side exhaust outlets.
- Improve the original Cardin ribbon/headliner treatment and front-fender emblems.
- Keep gameplay tuning distinct from verified factory specifications.
- Rerun geometry, import and native visual checks after the resulting changes.

Production totals and claims of 1974 Cardin availability remain unverified here. The registry's reconstructed press-kit HTML is not treated as an intact original scan. No outside photograph, brochure pixel or donor-mod asset is included in the mod.
