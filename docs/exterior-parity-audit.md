## Four fresh comparisons

### Comparison 1: full silhouette and front volume

Compared the saved source side/front-quarter and fresh side clay, front-quarter clay and target-frame-2 estimate against target frames 2/30 and the supplemental side/front photographs. The saved model retains a narrow, sustained longitudinal fender ridge, while the photographs show a broad shoulder rolling outward toward the arch. The front-third elevation in side view is also a conspicuous isolated hump. The overall measured wheelbase is not the cause. The current renderer exaggerates some narrow highlights, but the clay still shows the ridge.

Keep the grille, wheel centers, hood seam and A-pillar anchors fixed while adjusting `shoulder`, `side_top`, `hood_base` and `front_fender_point` together (`tools/build_mod.py:265`, `:272`, `:379`, `:384`). Do not increase the entire fender height or indiscriminately widen the car. Broaden the transverse roll, soften its inner control slope and compare the front crown against both front-quarter views and the side silhouette. A first trial can test a modest 10-20mm longitudinal crest reduction, but that is a reversible modeling hypothesis, not a recovered factory measurement. The off-axis target camera remains a gating uncertainty before accepting the amount.

### Comparison 2: roof, quarter light, sail and haunch

Compared fresh sail-close clay, rear-quarter clay, top clay and side clay against the supplemental side and Druk 032, then checked front target context. The roof has volume, but a visible fan/dent concentrates at the quarter-window aft tip, and the sail-to-haunch transition contains broad angular reflection patches. The curved glass perimeter is densely sampled, yet its surrounding surface still converges into a pointed boundary with a strong tangent change. This is visible with neutral clay, so paint does not account for it.

Review `quarter_controls` (`build_mod.py:404`), `surround` (`:508`) and `sail` (`:545`) as one shared surface. Preserve the existing exact perimeter positions and make transverse derivatives agree across the tip station, rather than adding another overlapping patch. Inspect the source boundary normal and mesh spacing before increasing subdivisions. The window outline reads more vertically rounded and shorter aft than the supplemental broad swept opening; that silhouette difference requires a side-camera fit before moving `QUARTER_TIP` or the door divider. Druk's vinyl roof boundary is equipment-specific and must not become a painted geometry crease on the target.

### Comparison 3: rear sheet metal, spoiler and optical parts

Compared the freshly saved rear-quarter paint/clay and current studio rear against target frame 22, Druk 034 and BaT 011. The revised crowned lid and oval latch cup are recognizable progress, but do not make the rear complete. The fascia, lenses and bumper still read as separate flat strips. The reference lamp cavities have rolled bright surrounds and visibly shaped reflective lenses. The model's red lenses and cream inserts remain nearly planar rounded plates. The red lower valance is a broad capsule-like extrusion with bulbous end corners instead of the tighter sheet-metal fold under the bumper.

Prioritize lens geometry and lower-valance cross-section before another spoiler-height change. `tools/javelin_rear.py:117` builds the lamp fascia, with flat lens plates near the end of `build_rear`. Give lenses a shallow convex profile, inset inner reverse lenses, and restrained original optical ribs. Preserve independent native tail/brake atlas zones. Replace the generic `body.rounded_plate((0,TAIL_FACE+.074,.405),(1.69,.15,.39),...)` at `build_mod.py:585` with a sampled folded lower valance whose edges join the quarter ends. `build_rear_bumper` already has a wrap and notch, but its six-point section still creates broad planar reflections. Round the cross-section at the highlight rolls before altering its envelope. Low studio reflection detail is partly lighting/material, so validate geometry with a moving highlight or alternate neutral light.

### Comparison 4: identity details, doors, wheels and attachment

Compared front close-up target frames 08/12, rocker frame 16, target fronts 2/30, current source and fresh fender close clay. This review found a concrete placement bug: the two intended windshield-wiper beams at `build_mod.py:710` use Z=.807/.870, below the windshield foot Z=.900 and nearby hood. They are buried. The three bars per side on the moving hood are vents, not a substitute. Put the parked wiper pivots, articulated arms and blades onto the exposed fixed cowl/screen surface, using its actual sampled coordinates with clearance. Recheck opening hood clearance.

Door handles at `build_mod.py:661` are generic protruding chrome pills. The reference has a rectangular recessed bezel, push-button/release detail and grip recess. Build this assembly on the door surface rather than increasing the existing pill. Side markers are simple boxes and need thin bright surrounds and recessed lenses. Front medallions currently contain only large AMO lettering; the target clearly includes perimeter text and differentiated center backgrounds. These can use original vector artwork after body geometry, without copying photography into the atlas.

The target wheel evidence available here is insufficient to infer an exact manufacturer or measured offset. `tools/javelin_wheel.py` has genuine dished five-spoke volume, so do not replace it with the supplemental steel wheels. The present tire sidewall and stroke lettering remain approximations. Compare against a sharper target wheel frame before changing spoke width or rim diameter. Preserve the newly corrected close-fitting single-inlet sidepipes; frame 16 confirms the guard and upper clearance are separate from the sill and no red runner belongs above them.
