## Analysis 1: independent whole-source review

**Coverage:** C article plus C05/C013/C03/Cfront and gallery grille, wheel, badge and second-car details. B article plus B1/B2/B3/B5. D listing plus152997/152799/152800/152893/152900. All three current exterior model renders were inspected.

**Conceptcarz:** The side and front photographs establish separate rounded front crowns and rising rear haunches, with a low arched roof and broad sail panels. Rear lamps are separate recessed units, and the bumper and spoiler have substantial curved surfaces. The red example's painted roof, white hood marking and black rear panel belong to that example. Other gallery cars differ, including louvers on one car. No inspected photo establishes its Cardin cabin. The article contains inconsistent production and engine figures, so its numerical claims are not a sufficient factory authority.

**Barn Finds:** Front, side and rear photographs show continuous rounded body volumes, thin bright window trim, black vinyl roof, slotted wheels, chrome rocker trim and a red rear lamp panel. The cabin shows a patterned instrument fascia, console selector, armrest and compact upper door insert. These visible features support shape analysis. The article's304/automatic, originality and ownership account does not specify the video car. Druk signage in B1 raises a source-independence concern.

**Druk:** Side and rear views make the deep rear haunch, inset rectangular handle, central plate recess and rounded spoiler perimeter particularly clear. Seat and headliner photographs establish nested rounded ribbons fitted to padded surfaces. The roof pattern surrounds a plain centre with a dome lamp. The listing's identity spelling varies. Its photographs provide stronger visual evidence than its sales language.

**Model assessment:** Front crowns still read as narrow ridges; sail/deck transitions remain segmented; window borders appear heavy. The front face looks tall and planar, the bumper rectangular, and the spoiler abrupt in side view. Camera mismatch prevents numeric proportion corrections. Five-spoke wheels, side pipes and the red tail panel cannot be rejected merely because supplemental cars differ. This review did not inspect a current model cabin render or fresh video frames.

## Analysis 2: independent whole-source re-examination

**Coverage:** C article and C05/C013/C014/C03. B article and all five car photos. D listing and152799/152800/152893/152900. All three current exterior model renders were inspected. Thirteen source photos were visually reviewed.

**Conceptcarz:** The roof, sail and rear haunch connect through changing curvature, with a long door and inset bright handle. The rear views resolve bowl-like lamp surrounds and a pronounced spoiler depression. Different wheels, exhaust location, tail-panel paint and hood graphics must remain separate from the target. Its cabin prose is not equivalent to a photographed Cardin interior, and conflicting specification figures cannot settle performance or production questions.

**Barn Finds:** Body and cabin photographs show the same main shape family with a vinyl-covered roof and different equipment. The dashboard is a broad assembly with recessed instruments, a three-spoke wheel and a separate console selector. The engine bay is crowded with accessories and hoses, rather than a few isolated primitives. The underdash audio unit is a useful visual identity marker. Claimed originality does not make it a blueprint for the target.

**Druk:** High-resolution upholstery shows soft narrow seatbacks, perimeter welting, broad pale ribbons and narrower warm-coloured borders. Rear and headliner patterns require their own layouts. The straight rear view supports a red panel, recessed lamp units, curved bumper and shaped spoiler recess. Matching vinyl roof, wheels, dealer context and underdash radio strengthen the inference that B and D show the same car or sales lineage, not independent factory corroboration.

**Model assessment:** The quarter window reads as a small rounded polygon rather than the photographed swept opening. Front crowns, sail and rear haunch remain too planar. Handles project as dark blocks instead of inset bright assemblies. Lamp rims and reverse inserts look flatter than the photographs. Bumper guards and spoiler recess remain simplified after the previous rebuild. Opaque exterior preview glass prevents an interior conformity claim. Exact window dimensions, spoiler angle and bumper section remain unresolved without camera matching.

## Analysis 4: primary-agent verification and model audit

**Coverage:** C article with C05/C013/C014/C03 and the already inspected Cfront; B article and B1/B2/B3/B5, with B4 checked in the full gallery contact sheet; D page text in the browser plus side/rear/seat/headliner/engine and the eighteen-image review contact sheet. Current side/front/rear exterior renders were checked. Retained seat and cockpit close-ups were compared with the current generator; these interior images predate the latest exterior rebuild and are not new native-game evidence.

**Conceptcarz:** Side and rear views support the large-scale body identity but do not solve our camera. The real side waist changes gradually beneath the sail; the model presents a pronounced triangular surface and a narrow shelf-like front highlight. Rear lamp bowls and bumper ends show actual surface depth, not just bright border colours. The source's white hood marking, black tail panel and additional gallery variants must stay outside the target specification. No photographed Cardin cabin was established from the inspected C views.

**Barn Finds:** The side and rear-quarter photographs confirm that vinyl roof borders can exaggerate apparent sail boundaries. They must not become artificial creases in a painted-roof model. B3 shows that the dashboard is a large contoured assembly with a shaped fascia, vents, controls and a console. Our retained cockpit render has three dials on a narrow flat strip and a generic wheel, so the earlier phrase 'cockpit checks pass' was only a clearance statement. The automatic selector and underdash radio belong to this example. The page's sales claims do not settle factory or target identity.

**Druk:** D152906 shows a shared rear bench with surface-specific rounded Cardin motifs. The later expanded gallery review clarifies paired compositions; the earlier wording of one broad continuous composition was too imprecise. The current generator loops over two lateral positions and two rows, making four similar individual buckets. This is a structural interior mismatch, not merely missing pleats. D152893 also shows more rounded, body-shaped front backrests than our tall rectangular pads. D152911/152904 establish slotted wheel spokes and a shaped instrument fascia. Wheel rotation alone cannot establish spoke angular orientation as a modeling error. D152900 shows the dome lamp and visors omitted from the simplified cabin. The VIN conflict was verified in the retrieved HTML; the later report adds actual label photographs.

**Verified conclusion:** Existing geometry passes are valid for their stated mathematical checks, but they do not verify the real car's shape. The most strongly supported remaining problems are the cabin/rear bench construction, sail and quarter-window contours, broad fender-volume transitions, inset exterior handles, and curved spoiler/bumper/lamp surfaces. A pale or dark preview reflection alone is insufficient evidence to alter chrome colour or glass opacity. Front lamp colour and supplemental mechanical equipment must first be reconciled with the video target.
