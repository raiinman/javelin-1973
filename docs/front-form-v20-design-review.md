Analysis only. No geometry changes or new acceptance. All sections remain open.

## What the target requires

The front reads as one continuous rounded assembly. Broad upper fender shoulders rise and roll outward around the wheels while flowing into rounded nose corners. Hood shape retains controlled longitudinal relief and a subtle crown. The grille surround, lamps, thin silver trim and lower red apron follow that curved face. The black hood/fender artwork crosses real panel boundaries without looking like a separate raised nose band.

## What succeeds

- Donor offers a more connected long-hood, fender-valley and wheel-shoulder construction than the earlier authored narrow crest.
- v20 removes some excessive hood inflation and broadens the upper field. Its clay comparison shows a real form change.
- Thin trim, black guards, recessed red lamps and separate headlamp bowls are useful target-specific features absent or simplified in the donor.
- v20 grille is calmer than v19's bright, busy texture.

## Where the design drifts

- v20 over-flattens the hood/shoulder relationship. The model's upper surface reads as a broad tabletop with steep outer walls, losing the target's shaped, rolling shoulder.
- The inherited fascia anchors preserve a straight rectangular face. Broadening the hood above it does not recover the target's rounded corner transitions or unified nose volume.
- The enlarged lower apron reads as a deep attached slab in these views. The target shows a more integrated curved red return beneath its trim. Exact depth needs a fitted low view.
- Target grille is visibly metallic and legible; v20 goes from excessive glitter to a near-black field. Opening shape, surface orientation and material response need separate diagnosis.
- Outer wing graphic endpoints form abrupt angular hooks in the render. Their wrapping and spacing amplify the incorrect shoulder shape. Reshape support first, then redraw the contour against the target.
- Hood seams and upper fender crests read as hard rails in the render. Some contrast is lighting, but clay also shows a stronger channel/crest construction than the desired continuous surface.
- The donor itself is not ground truth: faceted creases, roof panel lines, rear treatment and equipment differ from the restored target. Wholesale donor adoption would introduce new errors.

## Correction order

1. Freeze v20 as a comparison checkpoint, not an accepted form.
2. Fit camera hypotheses to named target landmarks (wheel centres, outer lamp centres, grille corners, cowl and hood leading edge), retaining the complete frames. Separate unresolved camera error from shape error.
3. Review the connected hood, both wings, nose corners and apron in clay against both target views. Treat existing fascia/cowl anchors as compatibility constraints to challenge, not recovered dimensions.
4. Recover broad convex shoulders and continuous nose returns without restoring the narrow tall crests or adopting all donor ribs. Verify front, quarter and side together.
5. Only after that form gate, correct graphic wrapping and grille response. Keep roof/interior work paused during this front review.
