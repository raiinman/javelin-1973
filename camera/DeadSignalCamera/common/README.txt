Dead Signal Camera
Standalone native isometric camera controls. No external renderer or installer.
Full world rotation and tilt are not supported.
Enable through Mods; existing saves also need Load > More > Choose Mods.

Use +, -, Close, Normal and Wide for smooth native zoom within your configured game zoom levels. Native auto zoom still applies.
For native world panning, bind Pan camera in Options > Key Bindings, then hold that key and move the mouse. The toolbar displays your current binding. It never changes your existing bindings.
F7 hides or shows the toolbar. Right-click the world and choose Camera controls to reopen it. Move the toolbar by its title bar. Its position is saved for the current character.
Optional preset shortcuts are unbound under Options > Key Bindings > Dead Signal Camera.
Single-player development candidate for Build 42.20.4. Multiplayer and controller support are unverified.
